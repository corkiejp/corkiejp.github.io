## 2,200-Year-Old Celtic Coin Hoard in Hungary

There have been reports regarding the discovery of Celtic coin hoards in Hungary:

*   **Celtic Silver Hoard:** A **2,200-year-old Celtic silver coin hoard** was reportedly unearthed at a construction site in Hungary [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/archaeology/2-200-year-old-celtic-silver-coin-hoard-unearthed-at-construction-site-in-hungary).
*   **Specific Discovery:** Another report mentions a hoard of **more than 200 Celtic silver coins** discovered in Hungary, with excavations in Szombathely showing evidence spanning nearly 7,000 years of human activity [Heritage Daily](https://www.heritagedaily.com/2026/11/hoard-of-more-than-200-celtic-silver-coins-discovered-in-hungary/160098).
*   **Value:** One source noted a discovery of a 2,200-year-old Celtic coin stash at a Hungarian construction site, stating they were worth over $2,000 at the time of reporting [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/topic/Celt-people).

***

### Related Discoveries

While the search results confirm the discovery of Celtic coins in Hungary, other related archaeological finds were also noted:

*   **Saxon Gold Coin:** A **2,200-year-old Celtic gold coin** was found near Leipzig, Saxony, which was noted as Saxony's oldest known coin, suggesting ancient trade links [Artnet News](https://news.artnet.com/art-world/oldest-coin-saxony-metal-detectorist-2705435).
*   **Medieval Finds:** A separate discovery in Hungary involved a **14th-century silver communion set** and 70 silver coins found during research near Lake Tisza [Arkeonews](https://arkeonews.net/archaeologists-discovered-medieval-silver-communion-set-and-70-silver-coins-in-hungary/).
