## What sb.scorecardresearch.com is
It’s part of Comscore’s ScorecardResearch beacon, used for audience and advertising measurement.

Partner/privacy docs describe it as a tracking tag that collects page‑view and visitor behaviour data for market research and ad analytics.

Because it’s a shared measurement script, it’s normal for you to see it across multiple, unrelated sites (space.com, apnews.com, etc.).


# What it is

ScorecardResearch is a measurement program run by Full Circle Studies, Inc., part of Comscore’s market research group.

It uses tags like scorecardresearch.com / sb.scorecardresearch.com to collect digital consumption behaviour data: page views, online activity, video streaming, and related signals.

# Role in tracking / fingerprinting

The tag functions as an audience and advertising measurement beacon, often loaded via ad tech or tag managers alongside other analytics scripts.

Extension has seen WebGL fingerprint‑style calls (e.g. webgl.getParameter on a WebGLRenderingContext) while ScorecardResearch’s beacon script is active, so you treat it as a tracking/measurement vendor that can appear in the fingerprinting chain.
