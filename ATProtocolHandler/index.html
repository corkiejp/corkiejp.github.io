AT Protocol Handler

Open at:// links in supported viewers, app views, and AT Protocol tools.

New

Direct URL support

This page can now be linked directly to a specific AT URI by appending it to the page URL.

Example link format

https://corkiejp.github.io/ATProtocolHandler/at://did:plc:oio4hkxaop4ao4wz2pp3f4cr/app.bsky.feed.post/3minbwyggw22p

This makes it easier to share or bookmark a direct link that opens the handler for a specific post or record.
