EuroLLM-9B: An Open European Language Model

EuroLLM-9B is a large language model specifically developed to address the critical issue of European languages being underrepresented and underserved in the existing global open-source LLM landscape.

Key Technical Details

  • Size: 9 Billion Parameters (9B).
  • Goal: Comprehensive coverage of all 24 official European Union languages, plus 11 additional languages.
  • Development: Trained from scratch with a focus on multilingual and European language support.
  • Date of Report: The technical report was published on June 4, 2025, detailing the development process.

Development and Training

The model's development involved several key innovations:

  • EuroFilter: An AI-based multilingual filter designed to ensure high-quality and culturally relevant data for European languages.
  • EuroBlocks-Synthetic: A novel synthetic dataset used during post-training to further enhance language coverage across European tongues.
  • Openness: The project emphasizes open research, releasing the base and instruction-tuned models, along with the EuroFilter and synthetic dataset.

Access and Availability

The model is available for community adoption:

  • Technical Report: Provides the full architectural and training overview. University of Edinburgh Technical Report (DOI: 10.48550/arXiv.2506.04079)
  • Model Repository: The GGUF version is hosted for open-source local inference. Hugging Face Model Page (bartowski/EuroLLM-9B-Instruct-GGUF)
  • General Information: General information and resources can be found on the EuroLLM website. EuroLLM Official Site
